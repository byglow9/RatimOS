    **1. `tools/curate_termo_words.py` (new, dev-time only).** Takes `--source <path>` pointing at a
    local checkout or download of the `fserb/pt-br` lexicon, `--out-c` and `--out-h` for the generated
    C, and `--answers-max <N>` (default 600) capping the answer pool. Pipeline:
    (a) read every lexical entry; (b) keep only entries of exactly five letters after normalising
    Portuguese diacritics to their unaccented ASCII base, since the game's tile grid and keyboard are
    unaccented — record the normalisation in a comment because it means `AVIAO` and `AVIAO`-with-tilde
    collapse to one entry; (c) drop anything containing a non-letter, anything capitalised in the
    source (proper nouns), and anything the source marks as a conjugated verb form; (d) rank what
    remains by the lexicon's bundled inverse-corpus-frequency score and keep the most common
    `--answers-max` as the **answer pool**; (e) keep the full filtered five-letter set as the wider
    **accepted-guess list** so the player can guess a legal-but-uncommon word without being told it
    is not a word.

    The script writes an intermediate `assets/wordlists/termo_answers.txt` and
    `assets/wordlists/termo_accepted.txt` for human review before generating C — the manual pass
    below operates on those files.

    **2. Manual quality pass (required, not optional).** Read `termo_answers.txt` end to end and
    delete every entry that is profane, a slur, medical or morbid, or otherwise likely to land badly
    as a daily puzzle for the recipient. A frequency filter does not remove these — only a human pass
    does. Record in `assets/wordlists/README.md` that this pass was performed, roughly how many
    entries were removed, and that it must be repeated whenever the pool is regenerated.

    **3. Generate `src/ratimos/apps/jogos/termo_words.c` / `.h`.** The header declares
    `#define RATIMOS_TERMO_WORD_LEN 5`, `size_t ratimos_termo_answer_count(void);`,
    `const char * ratimos_termo_answer_at(size_t index);` (returning `NULL` for an out-of-range
    index), and `bool ratimos_termo_is_accepted_guess(const char * word);` (case-insensitive,
    length-checked, binary search over a sorted array). The generated `.c` holds both arrays as
    file-scope `const char [][RATIMOS_TERMO_WORD_LEN + 1]` with the sorted accepted list, plus the
    project's `GERADO por tools/curate_termo_words.py ... nao editar a mao` header comment naming the
    source lexicon and its MIT license.

    **4. Record provenance.** Add a row to `docs/visual-identity/README.md`'s provenance table for the
    word data: asset id, source (`fserb/pt-br`), license (MIT), generator (`tools/curate_termo_words.py`),

    **Fallback.** If the source lexicon cannot be obtained or its structure differs from what RESEARCH
