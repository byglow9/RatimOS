    **Grid.** Build the letter cells with LVGL Grid layout at roughly 30px per cell for a 9x9 puzzle
    (scale down proportionally for 11x11), sized from the puzzle's own `grid_w`/`grid_h` rather than a
    hardcoded 9. A non-cell coordinate renders as a solid `RATIMOS_COLOR_BG` block with no border; a
    letter cell renders as a `RATIMOS_COLOR_PANEL` box showing the entered letter in
    `RATIMOS_COLOR_TEXT`, with its clue number, when non-zero, as a tiny superscript label in
    `RATIMOS_COLOR_TEXT_MUTED` in the top-left corner. The selected cell fills with
    `RATIMOS_COLOR_PANEL_ACTIVE` and takes a 2px `RATIMOS_COLOR_ACCENT` border; every other cell of
    the active entry takes a lighter `RATIMOS_COLOR_PANEL_ACTIVE` fill so the current word reads as a
    unit. Tapping a cell that is already the selected cell toggles the active orientation between
    across and down when both entries exist there.

    **Contextual clue strip.** A fixed-height strip of roughly 40px directly above the grid showing
    only the active entry's clue, formatted as the clue number, the direction word (`horizontal` or
    `vertical`), and the clue text, wrapped with `LV_LABEL_LONG_MODE_WRAP`. Beside it a small
    `proxima palavra` affordance calls `ratimos_cruzadinha_next_unsolved()` and moves the selection to
    that entry's first cell. This is the UI-SPEC's resolution of the vertical-space squeeze — do not
    render a permanently visible full clue list next to the grid.

    **Full clue list.** A `ver todas as dicas` pill opens a scrollable overlay listing every entry as
    clue number, direction, and clue text, grouped into `horizontais` and `verticais`, with each row
    tappable to jump to that entry and close the overlay. This satisfies JOGOS-04's literal
    numbered-clue requirement for a player who wants to browse.
